# Getting Started - Site Manager Guide

Welcome to Luna, your photography portfolio and client management system!

This guide will help you get started with managing your website, galleries, bookings, and client deliveries.

## Accessing Your Admin Dashboard

### Logging In

1. Visit your website and click **"Login"** in the navigation
2. Or go directly to: `https://yourwebsite.com/login`
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click **"Sign In"**

You'll be taken to your admin dashboard, where you can manage everything.

### Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your dashboard with:

- **Quick Stats**: Overview of galleries, images, inquiries, and bookings
- **Quick Actions**: Shortcuts to common tasks
- **Navigation Sidebar**: Access to all admin features

## Dashboard Overview

### Quick Stats

The top of your dashboard shows:

- **Total Galleries**: Number of public portfolio galleries
- **Total Images**: Total photos across all galleries
- **New Inquiries**: Contact form submissions you haven't reviewed
- **Pending Bookings**: Booking requests waiting for confirmation

### Quick Actions

Cards with links to common tasks:

- **Manage Availability**: Set your available dates for bookings
- **View Bookings**: See and respond to booking requests
- **Upload Images**: Add photos to galleries
- **Manage Galleries**: Create and edit portfolio galleries
- **Client Galleries**: Create private galleries for clients

## Navigation Sidebar

The left sidebar provides access to all features:

### Portfolio Management

- **Galleries**: Manage public portfolio galleries
- **Upload**: Add photos to galleries

### Client Management

- **Client Galleries**: Private photo delivery for clients
- **Bookings**: Session booking requests
- **Availability**: Set available dates/times

### Communications

- **Inquiries**: Contact form submissions

## Common Tasks Quick Reference

### Add Photos to Portfolio

1. Click **"Upload"** in sidebar
2. Select a gallery
3. Drag and drop images or click to browse
4. Wait for upload to complete

### Respond to Booking Request

1. Click **"Bookings"** in sidebar
2. Review booking details
3. Click **"Confirm"** or **"Decline"**
4. Client receives email notification

### Create Client Gallery

1. Click **"Client Galleries"** in sidebar
2. Click **"Create New Gallery"**
3. Enter client email and gallery details
4. Upload photos
5. Share gallery link with client

### Check New Inquiries

1. Click **"Inquiries"** in sidebar
2. Review new contact form submissions
3. Update status as you respond

## Getting Help

### Common Questions

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Currently requires database access. Contact your developer to add a password change feature.

**Q: Where are my images stored?**
A: Images are processed and stored securely. You can download them anytime from the galleries.

**Q: Can I organize galleries in a specific order?**
A: Yes! When editing a gallery, you can set the "Sort Order" field. Lower numbers appear first.

**Q: How do I feature a gallery on the homepage?**
A: When creating or editing a gallery, check the "Featured" checkbox.

**Q: What happens when a client gallery expires?**
A: After the expiration date, the client can no longer access the gallery. You can extend the date anytime.
