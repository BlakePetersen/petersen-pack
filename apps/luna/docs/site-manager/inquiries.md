# Managing Inquiries

Inquiries are messages submitted through your contact form. This is often the first point of contact with potential clients.

## What are Inquiries?

When someone fills out your contact form at `/contact`, their message is saved as an **Inquiry** in your admin dashboard.

**Typical Inquiry Types**:
- General questions about your services
- Pricing information requests
- Availability questions
- Session type inquiries
- Portfolio questions

**This is different from Bookings**, which are specific session date requests.

## Viewing Inquiries

### Accessing Inquiries

1. **Click "Inquiries"** in the sidebar
2. See all contact form submissions

### What You See

Each inquiry shows:
- **Name**: Contact's name
- **Email**: Their email address
- **Phone**: Phone number (if provided)
- **Message**: Their inquiry text
- **Preferred Contact**: Email or phone
- **Status**: Current state (NEW, CONTACTED, CONVERTED, CLOSED)
- **Date**: When submitted

## Inquiry Statuses

Organize inquiries by where they are in your sales process:

### NEW (Default)

**Status**: Just submitted, you haven't responded yet

**Appearance**: Usually highlighted (yellow, orange, or badge)

**Action Needed**: Review and respond

**When to Use**: All new inquiries start here

### CONTACTED

**Status**: You've reached out to them

**Action**: Initial response sent

**When to Use**: After you've:
- Sent email response
- Called them
- Provided information they requested

**Purpose**: Track that you've made first contact

### CONVERTED

**Status**: They became a client!

**Action**: Booking confirmed or contract signed

**When to Use**: When inquiry leads to:
- Confirmed booking
- Signed contract
- Paid deposit
- Scheduled session

**Purpose**: Track your conversion rate and successful leads

### CLOSED

**Status**: Inquiry completed without conversion

**When to Use**: When:
- They weren't interested after all
- They went with another photographer
- They were just browsing
- Inquiry was spam
- No response after follow-up

**Purpose**: Clean up inquiry list, track lost leads

## Managing Inquiries

### Responding to New Inquiries

When a new inquiry comes in:

1. **Check Your Email**
   - You receive automatic notification
   - Client receives confirmation they submitted

2. **Review in Dashboard**
   - Click "Inquiries" in sidebar
   - Find the NEW inquiry
   - Read full message

3. **Respond to Client**
   - Email them back (use email address shown)
   - Or call (if they prefer phone)
   - Answer their questions
   - Provide information requested

4. **Update Status**
   - Click on the inquiry
   - Change status to **CONTACTED**
   - Save

### What to Include in Response

**Professional Response Template**:

```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for reaching out! I'd love to work with you on [mention their need].

[Answer their specific questions]

[Provide relevant information]
- Session types
- Pricing overview
- Availability
- Next steps

I've attached my pricing guide and would love to schedule a call to discuss your vision.

Are you available for a quick chat this week?

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Contact Info]
[Website]
```

**Key Elements**:
- Personal greeting (use their name)
- Acknowledge their specific request
- Answer questions directly
- Provide value (pricing guide, portfolio link)
- Clear call-to-action (schedule call, book consultation)
- Professional signature

### Following Up

**First Follow-Up** (if no response after 3-4 days):
```
Hi [Name],

I wanted to follow up on my previous email about [session type].

I have [dates] available if you'd like to move forward.

Let me know if you have any questions!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Second Follow-Up** (after 1 week):
```
Hi [Name],

Just checking in one more time! I'd love to work with you, but understand you might be exploring options or timing might not be right.

If you're still interested, I'm here to help. Otherwise, I'll close out your inquiry.

All the best,
[Your Name]
```

**After Second Follow-Up**:
- If no response: Update status to CLOSED
- Note reason: "No response to follow-ups"

### Converting Inquiries to Bookings

When an inquiry leads to a booking:

1. **Confirm the Booking**
   - Either they book through booking system
   - Or you manually create booking

2. **Update Inquiry Status**
   - Change to **CONVERTED**
   - Save

3. **Add Notes** (optional)
   - "Booked family session for June 15"
   - "Signed wedding package contract"

**Why Track Conversions**:
- Measure success rate
- Understand which inquiry sources convert best
- Track business growth

### Closing Inquiries

Update status to **CLOSED** when:

**Client Declined**:
- They chose another photographer
- Budget didn't align
- Timing didn't work out

**No Response**:
- No reply to initial contact
- No response to follow-ups
- After 2-3 weeks

**Not a Fit**:
- Outside your service area
- Service type you don't offer
- Not actually interested (just browsing)

**Spam/Invalid**:
- Obvious spam
- Invalid contact information
- Marketing/sales pitches

## Best Practices

### Response Time

**Speed Matters**:
- Respond within 24 hours (ideally within 12)
- Faster response = higher conversion
- Clients often contact multiple photographers
- First to respond often gets the booking

**Set Expectations**:
- Auto-response confirms receipt
- Mention typical response time on contact page
- "I'll respond within 24 hours"

### Professional Communication

**First Impressions**:
- Use professional email signature
- Proper grammar and spelling
- Warm but professional tone
- Address client by name

**Provide Value**:
- Answer questions thoroughly
- Attach pricing guide or portfolio
- Link to relevant gallery examples
- Make it easy to take next step

**Call to Action**:
- Suggest specific next step
- Offer to schedule call
- Provide booking link
- Make decision easy

### Tracking & Follow-Up

**Use Status System**:
- Update status after each interaction
- Know at a glance what needs attention
- Prioritize NEW inquiries

**Set Reminders**:
- Follow up if no response in 3-4 days
- Check CONTACTED inquiries weekly
- Follow up on tentative bookings

**Take Notes**:
- Add internal notes about conversation
- Track what you sent them
- Note their specific needs/preferences
- Reference for future communication

### Converting Inquiries

**Qualify Leads**:
- Ask about date, location, budget
- Determine if they're serious
- See if you're a good fit

**Show Value**:
- Share portfolio examples
- Provide pricing transparency
- Explain your process
- Build trust quickly

**Make Booking Easy**:
- Clear pricing
- Simple next steps
- Booking link ready
- Contract/invoice prepared

**Follow Up**:
- Don't assume silence = not interested
- People get busy, emails get buried
- Polite follow-up is professional
- Know when to let go (2-3 attempts)

## Common Questions

**Q: Should I respond to all inquiries?**
A: Yes, even if it's not a fit. Brief, polite response maintains professionalism.

**Q: What if it's obviously spam?**
A: Mark as CLOSED immediately, no response needed.

**Q: How long should I keep old inquiries?**
A: Keep for at least a year. They might return later or refer someone.

**Q: Can I delete inquiries?**
A: You can, but better to mark CLOSED. Keeps records for business analysis.

**Q: What if someone submits multiple inquiries?**
A: Normal if they didn't hear back. Consolidate, respond to latest, update all to CONTACTED.

**Q: Should I provide pricing in first response?**
A: Yes! Transparency builds trust and qualifies leads. Attach pricing guide.

**Q: What if they're outside my service area?**
A: Politely decline and refer to local photographers if possible. Update to CLOSED.

**Q: How do I handle inquiries requesting discounts?**
A: Professional response: explain your value, offer payment plans if available, know your boundaries.

**Q: Can I export inquiry data?**
A: Not currently built-in. View in dashboard or contact developer for export.

**Q: What's a good conversion rate?**
A: Varies widely (20-40% typical). Track over time to measure improvement.

```
- [Photographer Name] - [website]
- [Photographer Name] - [website]

I hope you find the perfect photographer for your needs!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Integration with Bookings

### Inquiry to Booking Flow

1. **Client submits inquiry** about availability
2. **You respond** with dates/details
3. **Client wants to book**
4. Options:
   - Send them to `/book` page to request specific slot
   - Manually create booking after agreement

5. **Update inquiry to CONVERTED**

### When to Use Each

**Inquiries**: Questions, information requests, general interest

**Bookings**: Specific date/time requests, ready to schedule

**Both**: Inquiry first (questions), then booking (ready to commit)
